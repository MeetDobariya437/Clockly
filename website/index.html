Try Clocky's Daily Expressions
Always on Desktop & Tray
Sits on your desktop background without cluttering your Alt+Tab or taskbar. Win+D and swipe-proof.
Office Geofence Verification
Integrated Windows Geolocator verifies proximity to company coordinates for reliable attendance.
Dynamic Mascot Expressions
Clocky changes expressions as your day passes—from energetic morning vibes to drowsy overtime fatigue.
Smart Shift Reminders
Nags after 10 AM every 10 min until you clock in, and from 7 PM every 10 min until you clock out. Stops immediately upon action.
Hydration & Stretch Alerts
Stay healthy with periodic reminders to drink fresh water, stretch your arms, reset your posture, and rest your eyes.
Windows Credential Locker
Zero tokens in plaintext storage. Your credentials and session tokens are encrypted directly inside the OS keychain.
First-Time Quick Setup (Windows)
1

Enter your office email and password. Your token is encrypted in Windows Credential Locker—no daily login hassle.

Ready to Go!
Clocky stays pinned on your desktop or system tray. Click Clock In when you start and Clock Out when you head home.
