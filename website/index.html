The Intelligent Reminder & Wellness Engine
Never miss a check-in or check-out again. Clocky keeps watch so HR never has to flag you.
⏰ Morning Guard
10:00 AM Check-In Reminder
If 10:00 AM arrives and you haven't punched in yet, Clocky sends a gentle reminder every 10 minutes until you check in.
Rule: After 10:00 AM • Every 10 min • Stops immediately when clocked in
🌙 Evening Sentinel
7:00 PM Clock-Out Alert
Finished your shift? Starting at 7:00 PM, Clocky alerts you every 10 minutes so you never leave the office without clocking out.
Rule: From 7:00 PM • Every 10 min • Stops immediately upon clock out
💧 Hydration Nudge
Drink Water Reminders
Long meetings and coding marathons can dry you out. Clocky prompts you to grab a fresh glass of water every 60 minutes.
Cadence: Every 60 min while working • Auto-pauses after shift
🙆 Mobility & Stretch
Arm & Shoulder Stretches
Loosen tight wrists, roll your shoulders back, and unbend your spine with quick micro-break stretch prompts.
Prompts: Overhead stretch • Shoulder rolls • Posture reset
👀 Eye Health Care
20-20-20 Visual Breaks
Look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds to prevent screen fatigue and keep your vision sharp all day.
Benefit: Prevents headaches, eye strain, and dry eyes
🛡️ Zero Disturbance
Auto-Stop & No Spam
The instant you clock in or out, all alerts stop. When your shift ends, buttons are removed and Clocky peacefully sleeps.
Behavior: Native Windows toast only • Zero window focus theft
