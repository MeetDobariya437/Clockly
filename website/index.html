First-Time Quick Setup
1
Install the App
Download and run Clocky_1.0.0_x64-setup.exe to install Clocky into your system.
2
Authorize Windows SmartScreen (One-Time Step)
If Windows Defender SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" on the first run, authorize the internal build:
Click "More info" ➔ Click "Run anyway"
3
Launch & Sign In
Open Clocky from Start Menu or desktop. Log in once with your company credentials—tokens are stored safely in Windows Credential Locker.
